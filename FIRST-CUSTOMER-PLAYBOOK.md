## The 7-Step Onboarding (Day 0 → Day 30)

### Day 0 — First Meeting (60 min)

**Goal:** Show, don't tell.

```
Agenda:
  1. Watch him work for 10 minutes (current workflow)
  2. Demo Veltra — his actual patients, his actual schedule
  3. Show Today's Brief → "This is what you'd see every morning"
  4. Show Patient Memory → "Never ask 'why are you here?' again"
  5. Show Messages → "WhatsApp, but clinical"
  6. Show Drug Interaction Check → "This could save a life"
  7. Close: "You're slot #1. $699/year. Cancel anytime."
```

**What to bring:**
- Live demo on iPad
- Printed one-pager (the 5 primary features)
- Founding Partner agreement (1 page)
- His login credentials (printed card)

### Day 1 — Setup (90 min, in person)

```
1. Create his clinic in the system
2. Import his patients (CSV or manual — usually 50-200)
3. Set his working hours
4. Add his staff (receptionist, nurse — give them logins)
5. Connect his iPad to the system
6. Set up his Brief — first 3 days of morning intelligence
7. Train him on the 5 primary nav items (10 min)
8. Train him on ⌘K (2 min — "search and jump to anything")
9. Show him how to message patients (5 min)
10. Set up his prescription workflow (10 min)
```

**The rule:** Don't show him everything. Show him the 5 primary items. Let him discover the rest.

### Day 2-7 — Daily Check-ins (10 min each, remote)

```
Day 2: "What did you open first today?" (verify Brief is working)
Day 3: "Did you message any patients?" (verify Messages adoption)
Day 4: "Any drug interaction alerts?" (verify prescribing flow)
Day 5: "What's missing?" (capture feature requests)
Day 6: "What's confusing?" (capture UX issues)
Day 7: "Ready to go live with all patients?" (full adoption)
```

### Day 8-14 — Silent Observation

```
- Watch his usage data (anonymized)
- Note what he uses vs ignores
- Don't contact him unless he contacts you
- If he goes 2 days without login → reach out
```

### Day 15 — Mid-month Review (30 min)

```
- Show him his own usage stats
- Ask: "What would make you pay $999/year for this?"
- Fix the top 3 things he mentions
- Introduce one feature he hasn't discovered (e.g. Family Health)
```

### Day 30 — Renewal Conversation

```
"You've used Veltra for 30 days. You've seen X patients,
sent Y messages, caught Z drug interactions.
Your Founding Partner slot is $699 for year 1.
Want to lock in years 2 and 3 at the same price?"
```
