# VELTRA — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 9, 2026
**Effective date:** July 9, 2026

---

## 1. Introduction

Veltra Technologies ("Veltra", "we", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of health information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect data when you use our healthcare operating system.

This policy complies with:
- **HIPAA** (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) — United States
- **GDPR** (General Data Protection Regulation) — European Union
- **PDPL** (Personal Data Protection Law) — Saudi Arabia
- **ISO 27001** principles — Information security management

---

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Patient Health Information (PHI)
When clinics use Veltra, they may enter:
- Patient demographics (name, age, gender, contact information)
- Medical conditions and diagnoses
- Laboratory results and vital signs
- Prescriptions and medications
- Allergies and adverse reactions
- Appointment history
- Billing and insurance information
- Clinical notes and documents

### 2.2 Clinic Operational Data
- Staff user accounts (name, email, role, permissions)
- Audit logs (actions performed, timestamps, IP addresses)
- Device and session information
- Usage analytics (anonymized)

### 2.3 Technical Data
- IP addresses
- Browser type and version
- Device identifiers
- Login timestamps
- Error logs (sanitized of PHI)

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Under GDPR, we process data based on:
- **Consent:** Patient consent obtained by the clinic
- **Contract:** Fulfilling our service agreement with the clinic
- **Legal obligation:** Compliance with healthcare regulations
- **Vital interests:** Emergency medical situations
- **Legitimate interest:** Security monitoring and service improvement

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### 5.1 We Do NOT Sell Data
Veltra never sells patient data to third parties. Period.

### 5.2 Sharing With Clinic Staff
Patient data is accessible only to authorized clinic staff with appropriate role-based permissions. Each access is logged.

### 5.3 Business Associates
We may share data with vetted service providers who act as our Business Associates:
- Cloud hosting providers (with signed BAA)
- Email delivery services
- SMS/WhatsApp gateway providers
- Payment processors (Stripe)

All Business Associates are contractually bound to protect PHI.

### 5.4 Legal Disclosure
We may disclose data when required by:
- Court order or subpoena
- Law enforcement request
- Regulatory investigation
- Public health reporting (as directed by the clinic)

---

## 6. Data Security

### 6.1 Encryption
- **In transit:** TLS 1.3 (all connections encrypted)
- **At rest:** AES-256 (database and file storage)
- **Backups:** Encrypted with separate keys

### 6.2 Access Control
- Role-based access control (RBAC) — 10 roles, 25+ permission flags
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) available
- Session management with automatic timeout
- IP-based access restrictions (configurable)

### 6.3 Audit Logging
Every action is logged:
- Who accessed what data
- When and from where
- What changes were made
- Logs are tamper-evident and retained for 7 years

### 6.4 Infrastructure
- Hosted on Vercel (SOC 2 Type II certified)
- Database on Supabase (HIPAA-eligible)
- Regular security penetration testing
- 24/7 monitoring for security incidents

---

## 7. Data Retention

### 7.1 Active Accounts
- Patient data: Retained while subscription is active
- Audit logs: 7 years (regulatory requirement)
- Backup data: 30 days

### 7.2 Account Termination
- 60-day data export window
- 90-day secure deletion after termination
- Certificate of deletion provided upon request
- Audit logs retained for 7 years (legal requirement)

## 8. Your Rights

### 8.1 Patient Rights (exercised through your clinic)
- **Access:** Request a copy of your health records
- **Correction:** Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your data (right to be forgotten)
- **Portability:** Receive your data in a machine-readable format
- **Restriction:** Request restriction of certain processing
- **Objection:** Object to certain uses of your data

### 8.2 Clinic Rights
- **Data ownership:** You own all patient data entered
- **Data export:** Export all data at any time (CSV, FHIR, JSON)
- **Data deletion:** Request complete deletion upon termination
- **Audit access:** Full access to audit logs for your clinic

### 8.3 Exercising Rights
Patients should contact their clinic to exercise privacy rights. Clinics should contact:
- Email: privacy@veltrahealth.co
- Response time: 30 days (GDPR requirement)

## 9. International Data Transfers

### 9.1 Data Residency
- Default: Data stored in the region closest to the clinic
- Saudi Arabia: Data stored in-country (PDPL compliance)
- EU: Data stored in EU (GDPR compliance)
- US: Data stored in US (HIPAA compliance)

### 9.2 Cross-Border Transfers
If data must be transferred across borders:
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) are in place
- Transfer impact assessments conducted
- Patient consent obtained where required
